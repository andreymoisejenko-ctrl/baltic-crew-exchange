How it works

Two sides. One controlled introduction.

The pilot tests a structured path from unused capacity or urgent demand to a direct company-to-company agreement.

For crew providers

1
Declare available capacitySkills, crew size, dates, geography and documents.
2
Receive relevant requestsReview project scope before revealing company identity.
3
Approve contact disclosureBoth sides confirm mutual interest.
4
Sign a direct B2B contractYour company remains responsible for its own employees.

For project companies

1
Submit a requirementScope, location, dates, standards and crew size.
2
Receive a qualified shortlistCompare relevant capacity without public contact details.
3
Request an introductionThe provider approves disclosure before direct contact.
4
Sign a direct B2B contractCommercial and project terms stay between both companies.

Compliance & trust

Designed around accountable companies.

Pilot checks and requirements will be refined with participating contractors. The platform does not replace project-specific legal, tax or safety review.

B2BLegal entities onlyThe intended model connects companies rather than individual workers.
HREmployer responsibilityThe provider remains responsible for its employees and employment obligations.
↔Mutual disclosureContact details are intended to be shared only after both sides show interest.
✓Project-specific checksDocuments, certificates, insurance and sanctions may require review during the pilot.
